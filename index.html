NMF tutorial · interactive figures

Muscle synergy explorers

Browser versions of the MATLAB tutorials that go with Ting & Chvatal (2011), "Decomposing muscle activity in motor tasks: methods and interpretation." Compare how principal components analysis (PCA) and non-negative matrix factorization (NMF) break down EMG data. No MATLAB needed.

  • Figure 5.2

    2D synergy sandbox

    Mix two made-up synergies into 2-muscle data, change the weights and number of points, and watch NMF and PCA recover the synergies side by side.

  • Figure 5.4

    Postural synergies

    16 muscles, 12 perturbation directions. Choose NMF or PCA and the number of synergies, then see VAF curves, synergy weights, tuning curves and per-muscle reconstructions.

  • Figure 5.6

    Walking synergies

    The same analysis for about 6.7 s of walking EMG, with activations and reconstructions plotted over time in a zoomable window.
