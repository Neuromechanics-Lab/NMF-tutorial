Walking Synergy Explorer

Processed EMG from 16 trunk and leg muscles over about 6.7 seconds of overground walking (724 samples, about 9 ms apart). Compare the muscle synergies that NMF and PCA extract from the same recording, and see how well each set rebuilds every muscle's activity over time.

How many synergies?

The GUI picks the smallest number of NMF synergies that explains at least 90% of overall variability (VAF) and at least 75% of every individual muscle's variability. Bars show 95% bootstrap intervals (100 resamples of the 724 time samples) for the real data and for data shuffled in time independently within each muscle. Click a column to choose that number.

Muscle activity

Each muscle's EMG, normalized to its own maximum, over the selected time window. Both methods try to explain these 16 traces with a few shared patterns.

NMF: MATLAB nnmf (multiplicative updates, 50 replicates) on unit-variance-scaled data, with each synergy rescaled so its largest muscle weight is 1, as in NNMF_stacie_May2013. PCA: MATLAB pca on mean-centered data. Results were computed once in MATLAB from example_walking_data.mat and stored in this page. VAF is the uncentered r² used throughout the chapter; r² is the squared Pearson correlation. Both are computed over the whole recording, not just the visible window.
