What to look for

  • When the data fill the wedge between the two synergies, NMF recovers the defined W1 and W2 (possibly in swapped order). PCA vectors are always orthogonal, so they cannot line up with synergies that are not at 90° to each other.
  • PC1 points along the direction of greatest variance and PC2 is forced perpendicular to it. PC2 can have a negative muscle weight, which has no physiological meaning for muscle activation.
  • Set one data set's weights near equal and turn the other off. The data collapse toward one direction and NMF no longer has the wedge edges to find; try Re-run NMF to see the solution change with the random start.
  • The percentages differ in meaning. NMF reports each synergy's share of the summed reconstruction; PCA reports each component's share of the variance about the mean.
